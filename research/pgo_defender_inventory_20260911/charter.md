# Defender inventory and observed usage, version 1

Declared September 11, 2026 before new observations. Descriptive / NOT IN MODEL.
No fitting, injury deductions, expected replacement quality or prediction changes.

For newly generated replacement-depth captures, retain the complete already
constructed defender identity/role/history/availability records. Inventory version
1 identifies this additive schema. Existing aggregates and unavailable-player
cohort are unchanged. Earlier captures without the versioned inventory are
unavailable for this study; never reconstruct or relabel them as saved inventory.

The unit is one named defender in a team's saved pregame inventory for one game.
Preserve the provider's depth positions and ranks. Multiple listed positions do
not create multiple player rows. A listed backup is not necessarily the actual
replacement, and defensive snaps do not establish assignment, talent or causal
injury impact. Rookies and absent prior histories remain unknown. Reserve roster
status does not identify an injury diagnosis; ACT does not prove health.

Admit only a hash-verified season archive containing an inventory that was
generated and durably saved before kickoff minus 60 minutes. Verify the source
bytes and reproduce the inventory from its captured roster/depth and official
availability packages. Check current game/team/provider identity and explicit
verified FINAL bytes. Latest observations cannot overwrite a prior snapshot.

Target source is the same 2026 nflverse snap-count CSV used by the September 11
injury usage study. Preserve a new download's exact bytes, URL, real start/capture
clock, status and SHA-256. Do not invent a publication timestamp. Target capture
must follow the verified final observation. Reuse the injury usage join's stable
PFR-to-GSIS identity, exact game/team/opponent, duplicate and clock checks by
projecting the full inventory into a detached copy. Missing rows remain unknown;
explicit zero defensive snaps is observed zero. Pending finals, missing inventory,
missing targets and invalid evidence remain separately counted.

No existing source, charter, capture or study attempt may be overwritten. New
reports use exclusive directories and preserve the selected pregame pointer,
current final-state pointer, target receipt, code/test/protocol pins and manifest.
All results stay descriptive. There is no model-promotion or threshold-selection
rule, and no public prospective accuracy claim is authorized by linkage counts.
