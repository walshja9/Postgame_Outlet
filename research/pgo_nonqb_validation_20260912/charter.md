# Non-QB injury validation: source admission and prospective observations

Declared September 12, 2026 before the new audit and automatic observation
implementation. The user authorized validating non-QB adjustments. Existing
forecasts, old research attempts and source packages remain immutable.

Question: can the saved injury and playing-role information support an isolated,
time-safe test of offensive and defensive non-QB absence burden against PGO?
This stage audits admission; it does not estimate injury weights or promote a model.

Historical population: the existing pinned 2013-2025 regular-season injury
files and schedule, with the separately qualified corrected player-identity
package explicitly distinguished from the original conflicting sources.
Report coverage for every season, including missing/unmatched records. Grain:
season/week/team/player injury record. QB and special teams are excluded from
any proposed non-QB feature, but their source counts remain visible separately.
Decision time T is scheduled kickoff minus 60 minutes. Missing modification
timestamps and reports revised at or after T cannot establish pregame availability.
A historical modification timestamp does not prove an archived source vintage;
that limitation remains REVIEW REQUIRED even for rows passing the timestamp check.
Missing reports do not establish full health. No missing player or role becomes zero.

Verify the existing saved availability-term diagnostic and preserve its negative
result. It includes historical QB losses, heuristic status probabilities and a
different role convention; it is not an isolated non-QB test. Do not rerun fitting,
tune weights or borrow its September 8 scenario coefficients for the current
postseason-based weekly model. Original raw identity STOP and later corrected
identity PASS describe different packages; neither establishes historical timing.

Prospective descriptive population: all named defenders in an original saved
version 1 or 2 inventory, using that version's exact membership. This is a
defensive collection stage, not coverage of offensive injuries. For each verified
final game select the latest durable, verified versioned inventory containing
that game strictly before T. Selection is determined solely by saved clocks and
game identity, never by the score, target availability or observed playing time.
Freeze the selected pointer and retain it on subsequent checks. A malformed
selected capture blocks admission; do not substitute a more favorable archive.
No original inventory means no admitted cohort, with the game explicitly excluded.

Target: subsequently captured observed defensive snap count. Reuse the strict
game/team/opponent/PFR-to-GSIS join in the existing injury-usage and full-inventory
readers. Exact final-source replay is required. Raw target rows are target-only;
missing rows stay unknown, and only an explicit valid zero is an observed zero.
Duplicate, invalid, late or unresolved identities remain excluded. Repeated
captures are not independent samples: one game/team/GSIS row per selected cohort.
Prior usage is experience, not talent; observed snaps do not identify replacement
assignments or establish how many points an absence caused.

Use the existing nflverse 2026 snap URL. Capture actual response bytes, HTTP
status, headers, start/completion clocks and hash. Preserve unsuccessful HTTP
receipts. Publication time remains unknown unless independently established.
Fetch only when an eligible final cohort exists, at most once per 24 hours unless
a newly eligible final was observed after the previous capture. A later release
may resolve missing targets but never changes pregame features or selected cohorts.
The scheduled season writer archives these records independently of forecasts.
An optional observation failure must not suppress primary grading or publication.

Primary descriptive metric: admitted player-game rows divided by eligible final
cohort rows. Report counts of games, unique rows, observed positive and observed
zero usage, missing targets, invalid identities and missing pregame inventories.
Pending games and absent offensive coverage remain explicit. Any successful join
is descriptive evidence only; predictive status remains UNAVAILABLE and numerical
forecast_adjustment remains null. This stage has no fitted model, random splits,
tuning, predictive accuracy claim or automatic adoption.

The next numerical experiment requires its own locked feature construction and
source admission: two offensive/defensive prior-usage burdens for verified
absences, excluding QB, guessed questionable probabilities and replacement-quality
penalties. Compare against the exact corresponding PGO baseline on identical
chronological games. Primary target is final home margin including overtime;
primary metric is paired margin MAE, with RMSE and every season, Week 1, Weeks 1-4
and later weeks reported. Preprocessing and fitting use prior training data only.
Historical 2018-2025 seasons are already inspected and remain diagnostic. A lower
MAE, improvement in at least five of eight seasons and a positive lower paired
season-block interval are diagnostic screens, never prospective qualification.
Before scoring a frozen prospective candidate, declare its evaluation endpoint,
practical improvement threshold and sample-adequacy rule. New England's rank is
not a success criterion. Insufficient timing or coverage stops fitting at this gate.

Required artifacts: executable source audit with pinned inputs, per-season counts
and exclusions; original fresh target receipt and strict replay; automatic
descriptive observation checks with archived cohorts and receipts; runnable timing,
identity, missingness, source-integrity and preservation checks; public plain-language
findings. Keep technical operation, descriptive admission and predictive acceptance
as separate claims. All old forecasts and accepted grades retain their original data.
