# PGO model and site update — September 10, 2026

The main PGO model remains experimental. Its current picks and W/L/T records predict the straight-up winner. Any victory by the selected team earns a W; the actual winning margin is evaluated separately as lead error. PGO does not currently publish against-the-spread picks or sportsbook lines.

The site separates ranking calculation and input dates from availability, results and automation checks. A compact game-day view shows saved picks, win chances, kickoff, lock and sourced absences. Older or missing checks stay visible. Long board tests now run outside the season publisher's shared lock; the publisher verifies that newer commits contain only admitted season updates before rendering.

The accuracy view reports winner records, average lead error, average combined-score error, probability accuracy and confidence points. Comparisons use identical eligible games for each measure. A correct winner can still have a poor score estimate. Probabilities added after lock do not enter pregame probability evaluation. Confidence accounting discloses late entries separately, and older models do not receive invented historical probabilities or score totals.

## Fixed model comparisons

The new Model tests section reports all predeclared alternatives, preserves original forecasts before the real 60-minute cutoff, and grades them as verified finals arrive. Tests created after the completed opener exclude that game from their prospective record. Existing main forecasts and earlier studies remain intact. No test automatically changes the main model.

- **Updating score totals:** four fixed rules were evaluated on the same 2,127 historical games. The current prior-season team rule averaged 11.04 points of total error. Updating with a four-game starting weight averaged 10.74; an eight-game weight averaged 10.76. Both passed the historical further-study screen. Source timing still requires review, and these reused seasons are diagnostic. All four rules now receive separate prospective tracking; formal review requires at least 150 paired games across 12 weeks. [Methods and evidence](https://github.com/walshja9/Postgame_Outlet/blob/main/research/pgo_totals_candidate_20260910/README.md).
- **Passing inputs and probability methods:** removing either the QB passing block or team passing block slightly worsened margin error. None of the six fixed probability variants cleared its improvement screen. All remain HOLD. The three margin models and six probability methods are saved separately for future comparison, with exact control replay and no reallocation of confidence points. [Methods and evidence](https://github.com/walshja9/Postgame_Outlet/blob/main/research/pgo_weights_candidate_20260910/README.md).
- **Injuries and defensive replacement depth:** the admitted historical inventory lacks dated historical depth and source-capture clocks, so fitting a numerical injury effect is blocked. The updater now captures current identities, provider positions, official report coverage, prior defensive usage and unknown replacement history. Usage is experience, not a talent grade; rookies are not assigned zero quality. Non-QB injuries remain sourced context, with no invented score penalty. [Admission and capture evidence](https://github.com/walshja9/Postgame_Outlet/blob/main/research/pgo_replacement_depth_20260910/README.md).

The existing penalty experiment continues under its original fixed definitions. Weekly rankings regenerate after every game in the week has a verified final and the required statistics are available. Scores can arrive before those statistics; in that case the site retains the last verified rankings and explains the delay. Earlier weeks and source captures remain archived. See [season operations](pgo-season-operations.md).
