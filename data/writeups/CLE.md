Cleveland was routed at home by Jacksonville, and the game confirmed the profile: no

## The case for a good defense, dragged down

Cleveland grades poorly, and it is almost entirely the quarterback. The defense is genuinely good and the line was rebuilt — but there's no passing game to reward any of it.

## Quarterback

Deshaun Watson (−2.0) gets a half-point bump after his best game of the year at Tennessee (72.8 PFF) as Cleveland put up 491 yards. He's still a bridge-level starter, but the offense around him is finally producing.

## Quarterback: through Week 1 2026 (historical)

Deshaun Watson (-3.0) now sits below the worst-starter floor — a deliberate call after a Week 1 that graded out dead last among quarterbacks. His surface line flatters him: a 97.7 passer rating (15th of 30 qualified passers) and 9.3 yards per attempt (5th) on a couple of chunk throws. Underneath it was the week's worst QB game — a 37.0 PFF grade (30th of 30), a 34.2 QBR (25th), three turnover-worthy plays (tied third-most), a lost fumble, and five sacks taken (tied for the most) while holding the ball 3.4 seconds a throw and going down on a third of his pressures. This is his first game in roughly 23 months, back from two surgeries on a torn Achilles and layered on a pre-injury decline. He's the confirmed starter over Shedeur Sanders — but if this level of play continues, the calls for Sanders to take over will be deafening.

## What moved the number

- **Offense (-1.0):** the trench work is real — the **offensive line was genuinely rebuilt** (Fano, Zion Johnson, Elgton + Teven Jenkins, Tytus Howard). The receiver room isn't finished — Jerry Jeudy isn't a true WR1 — but Jeudy alongside **two promising rookies (Denzel Boston and KC Concepcion)** gives it real upside. There's still no quarterback play to cash any of it in, and the backfield is unproven — though Quinshon Judkins' quiet day (33 rush yds) is less on him than on the opponent: **Jacksonville had the NFL's No. 1 run defense in 2025, allowing just 85.6 yards a game**. A good line and a promising young skill group, held back by a broken passing game, nets out below average.
- **Defense (+0.5):** still the part worth taking seriously — the Garrett-to-LAR trade was offset by additions like **edge Jared Verse**, and **Denzel Ward** smothered his side (2 catches, 4 yards allowed, a forced fumble). Trimmed from +1.0 after Week 1, though: Trevor Lawrence went 18-of-23 for 4 TDs and three Browns defenders allowed a 150-plus passer rating in coverage. One game against a good offense — a nudge, not a plunge, and another like it drops it further. **Jeremiah Owusu-Koramoah (JOK) is out for all of 2026 with a neck injury**, already absorbed here.

## What changed — Week 1 2026

- **Watson -2.5 → -3.0:** graded dead last among Week 1 QBs (37.0) with a 34.2 QBR (25th of 30); the 97.7 passer rating overstates it. A benching for Shedeur Sanders is the next real move to watch, not just another rough line.
- **Defense +1.0 → +0.5:** secondary carved up by Jacksonville (Lawrence 4 TD); on watch for a repeat.
- **Offense held -1.0:** quiet (Judkins 33 rush yds, Jeudy 2/26) but not the culprit — one more dud and it drops.

## Bottom line

An above-average defense stuck behind the league's most negative quarterback situation. The line and Jeudy give the offense a pulse, but until there's competent QB play, the defensive grade is the only part of this roster to bank on.
