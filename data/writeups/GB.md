## Week 1: at Minnesota (L 22–39)

New season, same script: Green Bay led 22–10 late in the third and then collapsed, outscored
29–3 the rest of the way in a 39–22 loss. Jordan Love piled up volume (21-of-42, 387 yards,
two touchdowns, including an 81-yard strike to Christian Watson, who finished with 147 and two

thirds of it; this was self-inflicted, not out-talented, so the rating holds — the collapse and
Love's giveaways are the flag to watch.

## The case: talent on the sideline

Green Bay now grades below the line — not because the core talent vanished, but because so much of it is unavailable: Jacobs (exempt list), two starting linemen, Reed, and Parsons.

## Quarterback

Jordan Love (+1.5) is the starter, up half a point after his third straight quality game: 276 yards, a TD pass and a TD run against Dallas (80.9 PFF passing), after a 78.2 at Tampa and a 94.5 against Chicago. The shaky early-season version is gone. Josh Jacobs's return gives him the run game he hasn't had all year.

## What moved the number

- **Offense (0.0, was +0.5):** the preseason +0.5 assumed Josh Jacobs and a healthy line. Instead **Jacobs is on the Commissioner's Exempt List and hasn't played** (no imminent return), **RT Bako-Bewele is out for the year** and **LG Banks is out**, and **Jayden Reed (neck) has no timetable**. The backs have run for 37, 63 and 17 yards. Golden and Watson are real, and **Tucker Kraft** is back from his ACL, but this is an average unit at best until the line and backfield are restored.
- **Defense (−0.8, was −0.5):** cut 0.3 after Atlanta ran for 242 in Week 3. The grade is deliberately conservative and **has real upside on a delay**. Green Bay added **Micah Parsons — but he's on PUP recovering from a torn ACL and misses roughly the first four games** (a genuine game-changer once he's back, targeted around Week 5). Until then, **Hargrave and Franklin anchor the interior and Xavier McKinney is an excellent safety**, but with Gary gone the edge is thin early. The number reflects the September version of this defense, not the one Parsons joins.

## Bottom line

Green Bay is below the line. Its quarterback is steadying,
but the offense is missing its lead back and two starting linemen, and the defense just lost
Edgerrin Cooper for the year while Parsons' return slides toward November. The number comes back
in steps: Parsons first, then Jacobs if and when the league clears him.
