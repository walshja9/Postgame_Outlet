Arizona still grades below the line, but less severely — Week 1 showed the offense and defense
are better than a bottom-of-the-league read, even with a stopgap quarterback.

Jacoby Brissett (−1.0) is the selected quarterback, held after Week 5. He threw for 292 yards and
three touchdowns against Detroit with no WR1 (Marvin Harrison Jr. was traded to Houston) and behind a
backup left tackle. He's a steady, low-end starter who has graded as a better bridge than the floor

## What moved the number

- **Offense (-1.0 → -0.5):** raised after Week 1. **Marvin Harrison Jr. is the building block**

ball, Paris Johnson Jr. anchors the line, and rookie Jeremiyah Love is carrying the backfield until
Conner returns. The worry has shifted to the defense. Without Will Johnson the secondary is thin,
and there's still no pass rush to cover for it.
