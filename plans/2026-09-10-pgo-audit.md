The current calculation explicitly sets the availability fields to zero (`pgo_season_model.py:269`). Historical serialized fits contain availability coefficients, so the precise limitation is **no adopted current non-QB injury/replacement adjustment**, not the absence of any historical availability fields.

Prior defensive snaps are descriptive playing time, not a pass-rush grade, backup quality or expected lost exposure. Rookies and missing history correctly remain unknown.
